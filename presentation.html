Milestone 1: Curriculum Modernization

From Upload PDF to Interactive Syllabus Mapping

The Legacy Approach (Uploaded PDF)
  • Teachers created offline Word files and uploaded a PDF to admin/syllabi.php.
  • The document was a black box—the LMS had zero awareness of what was being taught in which week.
  • Admin had a "Document" column with download buttons that often failed or held outdated versions.
Problem: No interaction, no progress tracking, no CILO verification.
The BlendEd Improvement (Interactive Mapping)
  • Removed the redundant "Document" column and file upload inputs in both Admin and Teacher edit screens.
  • Structured week-by-week database records with delivery mode, platform, outcomes, and materials.
  • Both Teacher and Admin can print the complete official syllabus on demand anytime.
Improvement: 100% database-driven, editable, printable, and accreditation-ready.
Milestone 2: Assessment & Tracking

Manual Grading & Student Progress Engine

Hybrid Grading Workflow

We streamlined assessment grading in teacher/grade_submissions.php:

  • Automated Scoring: Objective quizzes auto-calculate scores instantly upon student submission.
  • Manual Scoring: Subjective essays, projects, and lab code allow teachers to assign points with custom feedback notes.
  • Instant Notification: Students see their grades and feedback in real-time on their grades portal.
Granular Student Progress Tracking

Added dedicated tracking tables and background listeners:

  • `topic_progress` Table: Stores individual student progress per topic node.
  • Visual Indicators: Progress bars in student materials, assessments, and teacher monitoring screens.
  • Early Warning System: Faculty can identify students who haven't read materials before midterm exams.
Milestone 3: Cloud Engineering & Stability

Railway Cloud Deployment & Reliability

MySQL 8 Compatibility

Resolved ONLY_FULL_GROUP_BY errors globally across student and teacher queries with session mode handling.

Clean HTTPS Port Routing

Configured reverse proxy rewrites in .htaccess and config.php to strip internal ports (:8080) from public URLs.

Session Hardening

Eliminated PHP whitespace header warnings and protected all student sessions with CSRF tokens and secure HTTP headers.
